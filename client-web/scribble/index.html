        How to play

        Everyone draws on their own phone or computer. Playing on one couch? Open this page on the TV, tick This device is the TV and create the room, then everyone joins with the code on their phone.

        Roast Portraits

        You get a prompt about someone in the room, like "Sal on their worst first date". Draw it. Then you write a caption for someone else's drawing, and everyone votes for the best drawing and the best caption (not your own). 100 points per vote. In a blind round your page stays blank while you draw, and the drawings play back stroke by stroke at the reveal.

        The Forger

        Everyone gets the same secret word, except the forger, who only knows the category. Take turns adding one stroke each to a shared picture, twice round. Don't make it too obvious, or the forger will work out the word. Then vote for the forger. If they get away, they score 400. If they're caught, they get one guess at the word for 250; if they miss, every artist scores 150. Spotting the forger is worth 50.

        Doodle Brawl

        Pick one card from each hand, like "A grandma" and "with a jetpack", then draw your fighter and give it a name. Fighters go head to head and everyone else votes for who would win. The winner stays on for the next challenger. 100 per win and 200 for the last one standing. A tie is a coin flip.
