            How to play

            Everyone at the masquerade looks alike. Some of the guests are your friends, and the rest are the crowd. Move like the crowd so nobody spots you, and watch for anyone who doesn't.

            Controls

            Running and shooting both get noticed. Guests stroll, pause and turn, and the safest player does the same.

            Party Night

            A night at the mansion: each room is a different game. Points come from winning, takedowns and surviving. Whoever is in last place picks the next room, and each round's winner claims the room they won. Between rounds, spend tips in the shop. The top two face off in the Last Dance.

            Playing on one screen

            Plug in a controller for each extra player and press A on it in the lobby. Nobody is marked on a shared screen, so your role and secrets come through your controller's rumble: one long buzz means you have the special role.
