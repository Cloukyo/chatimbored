Pile‑Up!

Tiny cars. One screen. Don't fall off the back.

Room

    Same screen? Press A on a controller, or Enter for a second player on the arrow keys, to add a driver. Empty seats are filled with bots.

    Track

      Waiting for the host.

      How to play

      Everyone races on one shared screen that follows whoever is in front. Drop off the edge of the screen and you have two seconds to get back on before your car blows up. Fall off the track and you're dropped back on behind. The last car left gets 2 points, the last one knocked out gets 1, and the first one out loses 1. The next round starts right where that one ended. First to 10 wins.

      Hit the gas just as the countdown ends for a turbo start. Rev too early and you stall.

      Grab the ? crates for one of nine weapons. Homing missiles, the machine gun and the mortar lock on: the laser sight turns red when you have someone. The shotgun fires out of both sides. Mines, drum bombs, oil and the flamethrower are for whoever is behind you. The flash bang blinds everyone, you included. Knocked out? Steer the crosshair and fire to call in air strikes until the round ends. Late in a round the screen starts to close in, so somebody always goes.
